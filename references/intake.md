# Intake

Extract existing answers before asking. Continue directly when brief is sufficient. Group only missing decision-changing questions:

1. What exactly do you sell? Include use cases, specifications that decide fit, and claims you can prove.
2. Who buys it, and which buyer roles or company types should be excluded? Give examples of good and bad customers if available.
3. Where should buyers operate? Include location boundaries and languages when relevant.
4. What commercial constraints rule buyers in or out: minimum deal size, capacity, lead time, certifications, or restrictions? Mark unknowns; do not invent.
5. Which current customers, competitors, and company aliases must be excluded?
6. Who sends outreach, which public routes are preferred, what contact details/approved copy may be used, and may plausible but unproven firms enter a separate exploratory tier?

Keep a concise internal brief. Ask a follow-up only if ambiguity changes a decision. Preserve brief version; do not paste it into every company row.

Before broad discovery, produce a short sector map: buyer definition, terms used by buyers, high-density sources, fit proof, and unresolved questions. Start independent research while a nonblocking answer is pending.
