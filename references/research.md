# Research and records

Use sources available to the current assistant. Check access once. Never require users to install a named tool. For a source family, log query, URL, date, names found, unique firms, qualified firms, and access state.

First map fairs, associations, registries, procurement portals, and sector directories. Deduplicate names before opening company pages. Verify promising names on company-owned sites or clearly attributable business profiles. Search web and social platforms for missing product or contact evidence. Inactive social pages are neutral. Do not claim broad social coverage from snippets.

Canonical records: company ID and aliases; source ID, URL and checked date; one observation per claim with source ID; public contact and source ID; decision with missing gate; draft with source ID. Keep raw excerpts out of contact export.

Four contact-now gates: identity/target market, acceptable buying role, specific offer/use match, public business route. Label fit exact, family, or needs confirmation. A directory/fair listing alone never passes all gates. `Explore` requires identity, route, plausible relevance, and explicit seller opt-in; keep separate. Missing evidence is held, not guessed. Clear mismatch, existing customer, and duplicate are excluded.

For each candidate send AI only decisive excerpts, offer constraints, contact candidates, and source IDs. Reject a positive decision whose citation does not exist. Revisit only when source, brief, or a missing gate changes. Parallel agents own disjoint query slices and append through one import queue.
