# Research and records

Use sources available to the current assistant. Check access once. Never require users to install a named tool. For a source family, log query, URL, date, names found, unique firms, qualified firms, and access state.

Use four source layers. First: fairs, associations, registries, procurement portals, and sector directories. Second: business directories and marketplaces. Third: company-owned sites. Fourth: public social profiles. Deduplicate names before opening company pages. Verify promising names on company-owned sites or clearly attributable business profiles. Use social for current activity or a public route, not as sole proof of buying fit. Inactive social pages are neutral. Do not claim broad social coverage from snippets.

Aim first for 20 qualified companies, not an arbitrary large list. Sample at least three available source layers before choosing candidates, unless fewer than three are accessible. Keep all candidates in ledger, rank contacts by evidence quality and fit, then deliver strongest 20. Do not inflate a batch with weak matches; if fewer pass, return fewer and identify missing proof. Pause after first batch for direction before widening search.

Canonical records: company ID and aliases; source ID, URL and checked date; one observation per claim with source ID; public contact and source ID; decision with missing gate; draft with source ID. Keep raw excerpts out of contact export.

Four contact-now gates: identity/target market, acceptable buying role, specific offer/use match, public business route. Label fit exact, family, or needs confirmation. A directory/fair listing alone never passes all gates. `Explore` requires identity, route, plausible relevance, and explicit approval for lower-confidence outreach; keep separate. Missing evidence is held, not guessed. Clear mismatch, existing customer, and duplicate are excluded.

For each candidate send AI only decisive excerpts, offer constraints, contact candidates, and source IDs. Reject a positive decision whose citation does not exist. Revisit only when source, brief, or a missing gate changes. Parallel agents own disjoint query slices and append through one import queue.
