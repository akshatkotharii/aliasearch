# Outreach and output

Personalization: one verified, business-relevant fact plus one precise offer reason. Avoid scraped-sounding observations, guessed buyer names, invented purchasing intent, false urgency, and unapproved sender claims. If a reference message is supplied, preserve its order and tone. Show sample drafts before scaling if style is unsettled.

Email: specific subject; normal greeting; relevant opening; sender identity and approved proof; one or two matched offer items; calm invitation to reply or discuss; real contact details. Social DM: shorter version, mention discovery platform naturally once, link to exact profile in contact view. Call brief: company activity, fit, offer angle, evidence, number provenance, buyer-desk question. For `explore`, ask whether relevant sourcing is handled there; never assert fit as proven.

Default user-facing columns: `Company`, `Location`, `Contact`, `Why this fits`, `Message`. Include `Subject` only for email and a clickable company or profile link only when useful. Put phone and email in Contact without repeating them elsewhere. Add fields only when needed for action. Keep held leads and internal gate details out of the default workbook; provide exploratory leads separately only if requested. For Excel, use a frozen header, filters, legible widths, wrapped text, accessible contrast, and functional links; verify the workbook opens and content is readable. If Excel creation fails, provide CSV and explain the fallback. Internal ledger retains source links, dates and decisions.

Measure human reply, qualified reply, sample/meeting, quote, and order separately. Do not infer deliverability or promise a conversion rate. Sending and inbox integration require separate authorization.
